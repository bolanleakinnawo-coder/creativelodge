Common questions

Before you get started

Do I need any editing or design experience?

No. The training is built for complete beginners — you don't need prior video editing, design, or AI experience.

Do I need my face or voice for any of this?

No. The entire point of the program is building a character that represents your channel instead of you.

What devices does this work on?

Everything is designed to be done from a phone or a laptop — no specialised hardware or software required.

Is this guaranteed to make money?

No program can guarantee income, views, or followers. Creative Lodge teaches the process of creating AI content and building a channel; results depend on individual effort and consistency.
